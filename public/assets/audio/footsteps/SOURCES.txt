Player footsteps

Every clip is a single recorded step, taken from the high-quality Freesound
preview of the source below. All sources are CC0 1.0 Universal (public
domain dedication, verified on each sound's page on 2026-11-19):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Processing (all clips): mixed to mono, high-passed at 70 Hz, resampled to
32 kHz, cut at the step's onset, tail trimmed 36 dB under its peak, faded
(4 ms in, up to 50 ms out), level-matched on the loudest 60 ms to -16 dBFS
RMS (peak capped at -1 dBFS), saved as 16-bit PCM WAV. No synthesis.
